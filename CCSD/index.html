About CCSD

Citywide Council for Seniors/Disabled

The Citywide Council for Seniors/Disabled (CCSD) brings together seniors and people with disabilities who live in public and affordable housing, so residents can share information, organize and speak up together.

We share these HUD resources in residents’ own languages so that more neighbors can take part in their resident councils. The translations are drafts made with AI and are being reviewed by native speakers.

To ask about trainings or printed copies, contact Sister Stephanie. To report a translation mistake, contact Angel.
